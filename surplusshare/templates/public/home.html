Simple 5-Step Process

How SurplusShare Works

We bridge the gap between commercial surplus and local hunger through an algorithmic, fair redistribution system.

Step 1
Food Businesses Post Surplus

Restaurants, bakeries, and grocers list edible surplus food, complete with safe expiry deadlines, portion quantities, and handling instructions.

Step 2
Community Pantries Request

Pantries and feeding programs submit food requests to category-based FIFO queues, guaranteeing fair, starvation-free access.

Step 3
Deterministic Matching

Merge Sort sorts items approaching expiry first, matching them with pending recipient queues. Volunteer routes are sequenced using Doubly Linked Lists.
