About SurplusShare

"Share More. Waste Less. Nourish Communities." — Uniting commercial surplus with local hunger relief through transparent technology and data structures.

The Challenge & Opportunity

Connecting Surplus with Need

In the Philippines, millions of individuals face daily food insecurity, while every evening, metric tons of wholesome, safe, and edible food are discarded by restaurants, bakeries, grocery stores, and catering establishments due to strict visual inventory standards or short remaining shelf lives.

SurplusShare was conceived as a university-level Data Structures and Algorithms final project and practical community platform designed to bridge this logistical gap. By applying deterministic computer science principles—specifically divide-and-conquer sorting and queue scheduling—SurplusShare eliminates human guesswork, prevents favoritism, and ensures time-critical food reaches community kitchens before it spoils.

Academic & Real-World Union
Developed in Python Flask and SQLite, the platform demonstrates how classic DSA structures—such as Merge Sort, FIFO Queues, and Doubly Linked Lists—solve life-critical community problems.

Our Mission

To provide a seamless, equitable, and transparent digital redistribution pipeline that empowers food businesses to safely donate surplus meals while equipping community pantries and feeding shelters with timely nutritional relief.

Our Vision

A Philippines where no edible food is discarded into landfills while families go hungry, fostering a circular, compassionate food ecosystem powered by open and ethical technology.

Impact Framework

Goals and Objectives

Our platform directly aligns with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

SDG 2
Zero Hunger

Strengthen food security by supplying high-protein, bakery, and fresh produce donations to frontline community kitchens and disaster-vulnerable barangays.

SDG 12
Responsible Consumption

Divert commercial food waste away from municipal landfills, reducing methane emissions and encouraging commercial circularity.

DSA
Algorithmic Equity

Utilize FIFO queues ($O(1)$) and Merge Sort ($O(n \log n)$) to prevent resource monopolization and guarantee fair partial fulfillment across all recipient pantries.

Who We Connect

SurplusShare acts as the central coordination layer uniting two distinct community pillars:

Food Donors
Artisanal bakeries (e.g. Sunrise Bakery), independent groceries (e.g. Green Basket), catering services (e.g. Harvest Kitchen), and produce markets (e.g. Fresh Fields).
Recipient Organizations
Community pantries, barangay feeding centers, charitable shelters, youth kitchens, and religious feeding ministries across Metro Manila.
Volunteer Drivers & Couriers
Community logistics volunteers who navigate optimized pickup chains sequenced via Doubly Linked Lists.

Our Food Safety Commitment

Food safety is paramount. SurplusShare strictly enforces minimum shelf-life constraints and storage instructions. Donated items must be within verified consumption limits and sealed in food-grade packaging.

  • Expiry countdown tracking updated every minute
  • Urgent warning badges for items nearing 6-hour windows
  • Immediate exclusion of expired listings from matching queues
