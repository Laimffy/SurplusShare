Global Commitment

United Nations SDG Alignment

SurplusShare actively advances two core United Nations Sustainable Development Goals through local action in Philippine communities.

2
Target 2.1 & 2.2

UN SDG 2: Zero Hunger

Goal: End hunger, achieve food security and improved nutrition, and promote sustainable agriculture.

In many Philippine urban areas, dietary deficiencies impact young children and elderly residents. SurplusShare channels nutrient-dense foods—such as whole-grain bakery bread, leafy greens (kangkong, pechay), fresh fruits (mangoes, bananas), and proteins—directly to feeding programs and community pantries that serve high-vulnerability populations.

SurplusShare's Tangible Contribution: Redirected {{ metrics.total_rescued_kg }} kg of fresh food directly to {{ metrics.recipients_served }} community organizations without charge or administrative hurdles.
12
Target 12.3

UN SDG 12: Responsible Consumption

Goal: By 2030, halve per capita global food waste at the retail and consumer levels and reduce food losses along production and supply chains.

Organic waste dumped in municipal landfills decomposes anaerobically, releasing methane—a greenhouse gas over 25 times more potent than carbon dioxide. By moving food into the consumption stream before expiry, SurplusShare prevents waste at the source and fosters a circular local food economy.

SurplusShare's Tangible Contribution: Achieved an estimated {{ co2_saved_kg }} kg in avoided CO₂ greenhouse gas emissions through rapid algorithmic reallocation.

Our Four Pillars of Sustainability

1. Food Waste Reduction

Prioritizing surplus with imminent expiry deadlines using Merge Sort algorithms to guarantee rapid matching.

2. Community Assistance

Supplying free food to frontline community kitchens, reducing budgetary strain for grassroots charity pantries.

3. Optimized Distribution

Chaining stops sequentially with Doubly Linked Lists to minimize volunteer fuel consumption and driving distances.

4. Business Collaboration

Partnering with local restaurants and grocers to improve their corporate social responsibility and environmental impact.
